Documentation Templates

Templates are the easiest way to enforce documentation standards. Everyone starts from the same structure, so nothing important gets forgotten and every document looks familiar.

How to use these templates

  1. Copy or downloadthe template you need. The templates are in Markdown, which works in Git, most wikis and many ticketing tools.
  2. Replace every [placeholder]with real content. Remove sections that truly don't apply, but think twice first.
  3. Start at version 0.1 with status “Draft”.The document becomes 1.0 only after review and approval.
  4. Store it in the agreed locationusing the naming convention.
